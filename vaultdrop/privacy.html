VaultDrop Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

VaultDrop uses account, contact, shipping, and transaction information only as needed to operate the app, provide card fulfillment, support users, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

Information we may process

This may include account identifiers, contact details, shipping address information, app activity, order and fulfillment records, and information returned by connected services such as eBay when a user authorizes that connection.

eBay connection

When an eBay account is connected, VaultDrop uses OAuth authorization. VaultDrop does not ask for or receive the user's eBay password. OAuth tokens and related credentials are used only to perform the actions the user authorized and to operate fulfillment and order-status features.

Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers only as necessary to operate VaultDrop, process fulfillment, deliver purchases, maintain infrastructure, or comply with law. VaultDrop does not sell personal information.

Security and retention

VaultDrop applies reasonable technical safeguards to credentials and fulfillment data and retains information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the VaultDrop operator through the contact method provided in the VaultDrop app.
