Private owner setup

Connect eBay to VaultDrop.

One secure setup form. We'll save your approved production eBay credentials directly to VaultDrop, then send you to eBay to authorize your buyer account.

Checking eBay connection…No purchases will be made during setup.
1 Get your production details
Open eBay Developer Keys ↗ Open Railway ↗

From eBay, copy your Production App ID, Cert ID, and Production RuName (under User Tokens). From Railway → vaultdrop-fulfillment-api → Variables, copy your existing VAULTDROP_SETUP_KEY.

2 Set your eBay redirect URL

Under eBay User Tokens → Production RuName, set the Auth Accepted URL to this exact address:

https://vaultdrop-fulfillment-api-production-0267.up.railway.app/api/ebay/oauth/callback
3 Connect the account
Owner authorization only. Never share this in chat.

What happens next: VaultDrop checks your eBay credentials, then redirects to eBay's own sign-in page. Approve access there; the browser returns to VaultDrop with the connection result. We never request your eBay password.

Does this automatically activate paid packs?

No. eBay connection is separate from purchase eligibility. Paid pack checkout and automatic eBay ordering remain disabled until the rest of the fulfillment, reserve, and payment compliance checks pass.

Important: only submit production credentials you control. The eBay Order API requires separate member-checkout authorization; a standard eBay developer approval may not include it.
