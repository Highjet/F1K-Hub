# NEXUS // ARENA — Illustration credits and licensing

The character paintings in the current prototype are sourced from the original WTactics / Arcmage art repository:

- Collection: https://github.com/wtactics/art/tree/master/WT
- Artwork license: Creative Commons Attribution–ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- Per-card painting filename: see `art-direction.json` and each card's in-game inspection panel.

WTactics project notes identify Santiago Iborra as the primary illustrator, with additional artists including Chris Wilson (Spider Woman), Nico Goeminne (Knight), and others. Use the original project credits and folder details for authoritative attribution.

The NEXUS card IDs, names, roles, ability labels, CSS/SVG scenic backdrop engine, and layout are prototype game-specific creations, not source names. Displaying these paintings alongside game-specific backgrounds/framing may constitute adaptations subject to CC BY-SA requirements. Preserve credits, license links, and the applicable share-alike notices in any distribution, and seek legal review before a commercial launch.

**Prototype caveat:** Images sourced from WTactics are temporary reference illustrations. The 40 individual art direction briefs in `art-direction.json` specify original creature designs and settings. Some reference paintings do not precisely depict those designs. Commission or create bespoke final illustrations matching each brief before asserting this is a wholly original, market-ready creature IP.
